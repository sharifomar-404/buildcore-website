OUR COMMITMENT

Contributing to a Stronger Future

Saudi Vision 2030 represents a national roadmap for economic diversification, development and long-term prosperity. It emphasizes innovation, investment, sustainability and the development of infrastructure across the Kingdom.

At BuildCore, we understand that quality construction materials and reliable building systems are important elements in delivering infrastructure and developments that meet the demands of the future.

THE NATIONAL VISION

Three Pillars. One Ambitious Future.

Saudi Vision 2030 is built around three interconnected pillars that guide the Kingdom's transformation.

01

A Vibrant Society

A society built on strong foundations, quality of life, cultural identity and an environment where people can thrive.

02

A Thriving Economy

A diversified and competitive economy driven by investment, innovation, business development and new opportunities.

03

An Ambitious Nation

An effective and responsible nation focused on strong institutions, accountability and long-term development.

Learn more about the three pillars on the official Vision 2030 website
